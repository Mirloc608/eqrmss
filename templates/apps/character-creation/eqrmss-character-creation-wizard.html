Character Creation Overview

EQRMSS character creation follows the Rolemaster Standard System rules adapted for EverQuest. You will proceed through basic info, race, class, origin, stats, and final review.

  • Enter name and gender.
  • Select race and class.
  • Choose origin city and deity.
  • Assign RMSS stats.
  • Review and finalize your character.
