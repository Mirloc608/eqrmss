Step 1 of 3 — Race

{{!-- STEP 1 — RACE --}}

The race is the level-1 template: base stats, hit die, and natural attacks.

{{!-- STEP 2 — ROLE / CLASS + LEVEL --}}

The build is simulated level-by-level with PC rules: stat gain rolls, then DP spent by the role's (or class's) purchase priorities.

A classed NPC buys as if it were the actual class. Non-sentient creatures use roles.

{{!-- STEP 3 — SPECIAL ATTACKS --}}

One special attack per 10-level band, drawn from what EverQuest creatures of this kind actually use. Leave a band on "none" to skip it.
